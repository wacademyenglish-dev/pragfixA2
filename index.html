Script Scanner

Click the highlighted words to analyze the trailer's script.

> AUDIO LOG_01 [ GIRL ]...

"What is this...?"

Data Verb TO BE (Present)

Used to ask about the nature of an unknown object or place.

Comms Prompt "Teacher, ask: What do YOU think that place is? Is it Earth?"

> AUDIO LOG_02 [ ASTRONAUT ]...

"Freedom. Our freedom."

Data Possessive Adjective

Indicates that the freedom belongs to "us" (the astronaut and the girl).

Comms Prompt "Teacher, ask: Why do they want freedom? Who are they running from?"

System Log

Threat Analysis

> TRANSLATING SUIT A.I. PREDICTION...

"Warning. The satellite is going to crash."

"We will evacuate immediately."

GOING TO:

Used here because the falling satellite is clear visual EVIDENCE in the present.

WILL:

Used here as a SUDDEN DECISION / Immediate reaction to extreme danger.

Combat Prompt "What are YOU going to do if a satellite falls here?"

Quick Time Event

Will vs. Going To

Act fast! Choose the correct future form.

Situation: You see a giant rock falling.

"Watch out! It ___ hit us!"

> is going to

Situation: The girl is scared. You make a promise.

"Don't worry. I ___ stay with you."

> will
